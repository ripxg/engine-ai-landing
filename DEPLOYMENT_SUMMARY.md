## 📊 Technical Specifications

### Bubble Animation Details
- **12 columns** of bubbles (columns 4-7 are denser for central focal point)
- **~440 total bubbles** (40 per central column, 20 per outer column)
- **Bubble sizes**: 1-4px radius
- **Speed**: 0.5-2.0 pixels per frame
- **Opacity**: 0.2-0.8 for depth effect
- **Outer glow**: 4x radius with gradient
- **Animation loop**: requestAnimationFrame for 60fps
- **Wobble effect**: Sinusoidal horizontal movement

### Color Palette
- **Background**: #0a0a0f (dark charcoal)
- **Primary cyan**: #06b6d4 (cyan-500)
- **Glow cyan**: #00dcff (luminous cyan)
- **Text white**: #ffffff
- **Text gray**: #6b7280 (gray-400)
